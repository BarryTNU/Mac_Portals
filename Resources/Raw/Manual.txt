## 1. Introduction

Portals is a desktop organization utility designed to make managing files, folders and applications easier.

It allows you to create individual areas, called Portals, directly on your Windows desktop. Each Portal acts as a convenient container for shortcuts to frequently used files, folders and applications.

Instead of having icons scattered across the desktop, you can organize them into logical groups.

Examples include:

- Work – documents and work-related applications.
- Finance – banking, spreadsheets and financial documents.
- Photography – image folders and editing applications.
- Personal – frequently used personal files and folders.

Portals saves your configuration automatically, so your desktop organization is retained between sessions.

## 2. Getting Started

When Portals is first launched, the User Manual is displayed automatically.

You can close the manual and begin creating your first Portal.

The manual can also be opened at any time from the application's dropdown menu.

Once installed, Portals runs in the background and does not interfere with your normal desktop usage.
Portals runs automatically when Windows starts, so your desktop organization is always available.

## 3. Creating a Portal

Creating a Portal is straightforward.
 
1. Click and drag your mouse to draw a rectangle around the area where you want the Portal.
2. Release the mouse button to complete the selection.

A new Portal will appear at the selected desktop location.

When the Portal is first created, it is given a default name. You can change the name at any time.

The default name is **New Portal**.
when a portal is first created the Name field is open for editing. You can change the name at that time or later.

### Naming a Portal

To change the Portal name:

1. Triple-click the Portal name.
2. Enter your preferred name.
3. Confirm the change by pressing Enter, or clicking outside the portal.

Your new name will be saved automatically.

## 4. Managing Portals

Each Portal is an independent desktop window.
Portals can be positioned on the desktop to suit your preferred layout.

## 5. Adding Shortcuts

Portals can contain shortcuts to files, folders and applications.

To add a shortcut:

1. Left Click and drag the Shortcut from its original location to the desired Portal.

The shortcut will be added to the Portal, and removed from the Desktop

The original file or application is not moved. Portals simply provides a convenient way to access it.

(Note>  Some Shortcuts store their icon image in the original file. If the original file is moved or deleted, the shortcut may no longer display its icon correctly.
If this is the case, you can manually change the icon image by right clicking the shortcut and selecting Properties. Then click Change Icon.)

### Removing a Shortcut

A shortcut can be dragged fron a Portal back onto the desktop, or into a folder.
If you delete a portal, the Shortcuts within it are returned to the desktop.
An exception is items which are links to files or folders.  These are not returned to the desktop. Instead they are deleted, leaving the original location unchanged.

## 6. Saving Your Configuration

Portals automatically saves its configuration.

This includes information such as:

- Portal names.
- Portal positions.
- Portal dimensions.
- Portal appearance settings.
- Shortcuts contained within each Portal.

When you restart Portals, your previously saved configuration is restored.

Your original files and folders remain in their existing locations.

## 7. Changing the Portal apperance and state.

Click the Menu box on the Title bar (3 vertical dots)
 The options are: 
    Keep Portal Visible
            By default, Keep Portal Visible is checked. 
            Click to Uncheck it.  
            The portal minimises to just the title bar.
            Move the mouse onto the title bar, and the Portal becomes Visible.
            When the Mouse leaves the Portal, It again becomes minimised.
    Change Portal Transparency
            Transparency can be varied between 50% and 100% (100% is no Transparency)
    Change Portal Colour
            There are 10 colour choices, Select a colour, and click Apply
    User Manual
            This Manual.
    Delete Portal
            Confirm Deletion, and all the portal's Shortcuts are returned to the desktop.

#Note:  The  Shartcut Right Click menu is not available when the shortcut is in a portal.  To access the Shortcut Right Click menu, drag the shortcut out of the portal onto the desktop.

## 8. The User Manual

You can access this manual whenever required.

Select **Manual** from the Portals dropdown menu.

The manual opens in a separate window, allowing you to refer to the instructions while working with Portals.

The manual is displayed automatically the first time Portals is run. It will not automatically appear on subsequent launches.


## 9. Tips for Organizing Your Desktop

For the best results, consider the following:

- Use descriptive Portal names.
- Group related shortcuts together.
- Avoid creating unnecessarily large numbers of Portals.
- Arrange Portals so that frequently used items are easy to access.
- Keep your desktop layout simple and consistent.

## 10. Future Features

Portals is an evolving application. Additional functionality is planned, including further desktop interaction and shortcut management options.

Features will be added as development progresses.
